## Why FLIP

- **Instant switching.** Every service stays loaded in the background, so moving between them never reloads or logs you out.
- **Find anything in a keystroke.** Press the backtick key to search every service across every workspace.
- **Plain YAML config.** One human-editable file with your comments preserved, live-synced to every open tab.
- **Latency-aware health.** See at a glance what is fast, slow or down.
